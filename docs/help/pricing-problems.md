# Missing tariffs and price differences

::: info
Source-reviewed training draft · TransferBook+ v0.7.9. Verify installed release, permissions and configuration. Use authorized synthetic training data; no live activation is implied.
:::

## Why is a manual quotation required?
The route or requested service may not have an applicable configured tariff. A zero placeholder is not a free journey. Quote-pending reservations cannot be confirmed or dispatched until approved.

## Why did the total change?
Compare route, service mode, vehicle class, date/time, extras, return discounts and configured seasonal, holiday or night adjustments. Review applicable tax and the server-verified breakdown. Compare like-for-like inputs rather than copying an earlier price.

## What should I do?
Use the current quotation and ask an authorized company administrator to resolve missing tariffs. Do not invent rates. If a stale quote is rejected, refresh the quotation through the application before proceeding.

## Escalation
Provide the service mode and sanitized input differences, installed release and error message. Do not include passenger contact details.
