# Help Centre

## I cannot create a reservation
Check your role, active operating hub, vehicle class, route and verified quotation. See [Create a reservation](/plus/create-reservation).

## The customer has no email address
In TransferBook+ email is optional. Enter the real contact telephone number and leave email empty. See [Passenger information](/plus/passenger-information).

## The price is missing or requires a quotation
An unconfigured route is not a free service. Follow the [manual quotation workflow](/plus/manual-quotations).

## I cannot dispatch a booking
Check status, pending quotation, assignment permissions, driver availability and capacity. See [Dispatch schedule](/plus/dispatch-schedule).

## An integration is unavailable
Check whether the service is configured and approved. Do not bypass disabled controls. See [Integrations](/integrations/).
