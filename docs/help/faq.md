# Frequently asked questions

::: info Reference release
Reference v0.7.26, not a live installation audit or commercial activation approval. Confirm installed version, permissions, configuration and acceptance.
:::

## Do staff need an application installed?

No native installation is required for browser access. Use an approved HTTPS deployment and supported devices; mobile compatibility and GPS acceptance must be tested separately. [System requirements](/system-requirements/)

## How do we create an administrator, dispatcher or driver login?

Request authorized backend provisioning with the minimum necessary role. A driver roster entry does not automatically create a login or link it to assignments. Staff use individual email/password credentials. Public applications do not create accounts. [Staff administration](/plus/staff-administration.html)

## Can a passenger book without email?

Yes. Use the actual passenger telephone and leave email empty if not supplied. Do not invent an address. This is different from staff authentication. [Phone-only booking](/tutorials/phone-only-booking.html)

## Which services can we configure?

The release supports one-way and return transfers, hourly chauffeur service and private tours. Configure applicable markets, locations, capacities and prices, then test each service sold. [First reservation](/getting-started/first-reservation.html)

## Why does a route require a manual quote?

A missing or invalid tariff is not a free ride. Review direction, time, class and applicable tariff rules. Use the authorized manual quotation process when a verified fare is unavailable. [Manual quotations](/plus/manual-quotations.html)

## Can different drivers perform the outward and return legs?

The release supports independent legs. Verify each leg's assignment, time, capacity, vehicle and progress separately. [Chauffeur workspace](/plus/chauffeur-workspace.html)

## Why was a dispatch assignment rejected?

Check the reservation and quotation status, full journey interval, driver duty or absence, vehicle maintenance, capacity and competing assignments. Refresh after a conflict; do not force the assignment. [Dispatch schedule](/plus/dispatch-schedule.html)

## Can I take partial cash and hand it over later?

Record only cash actually received using the authorized workflow. Review the remaining balance and collecting chauffeur. Confirm physical office custody separately after actual handover. [Cash management](/plus/cash-management.html)

## Are online payments already enabled?

No live provider activation is established for this release. Each provider needs approved merchant credentials, callback and endpoint validation, acceptance and authorization. Viva credential descriptions disagree; Skrill refund is unsupported in the inspected adapter. [Payment setup](/integrations/payment-api-setup.html)

## Can customers book directly from our website?

A separate gateway and publication workflow exist in source. Public intake remains disabled pending explicit approval. A website preview or active channel record alone does not enable public bookings. [Customer gateway](/plus/customer-booking-gateway.html)

## Does God's Eye track every driver and flight automatically?

The release includes a local geographic canvas, authorized on-duty GPS sharing and persistent manual flight/ferry observations. Licensed maps, physical GPS acceptance, automatic external arrival monitoring and scheduling remain outstanding. [Fleet tracking](/integrations/live-tracking.html) and [Arrival intelligence](/plus/arrival-intelligence.html)

## Are WhatsApp, email, SMS and AI already connected?

Adapter and conversational foundations exist; external transmission and provider use require separate configuration and authorization. A preview is not delivery; an AI draft is not a booking. [AI and messaging](/integrations/ai-messaging-setup.html)

## Can we outsource a transfer to another company?

Persistent subcontractor assignment and settlement workflows are implemented. Verify the approved supplier relationship, correct leg, agreed amount and actual settlement. This is not automatic external supplier dispatch. [Subcontractors](/plus/subcontractors.html)

## Does the system submit myDATA or EDXEIX documents live?

No. The documented release has persistent sandbox records and blocks live submission. Follow approved statutory procedures and obtain separate regulatory acceptance. [Regulatory sandbox](/integrations/regulatory-sandbox.html)

## What should I do after a booking or payment timeout?

Do not submit again blindly. Search for the existing reference and verify authoritative reservation or payment records. Escalate unresolved results to the authorized support owner. [Retry-safe creation](/developers/idempotency.html) and [Payment review](/tutorials/payment-review.html)

## How do we update or recover the system?

Use the approved release-specific procedure and verified backup/recovery plan. The protected staging updater is not blanket commercial upgrade authorization. [Software updates](/plus/software-update-manager.html) and [Recovery](/getting-started/backup-recovery.html)

## Does 100% onboarding mean we can go live?

No. Configuration readiness is separate from application acceptance, actual access tests, provider approval and business sign-off. [Go-live checklist](/getting-started/go-live-checklist.html)
