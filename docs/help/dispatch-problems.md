# Dispatch conflicts and missing assignments

::: info
Source-reviewed training draft · TransferBook+ v0.7.9. Verify installed release, permissions and configuration. Use authorized synthetic training data; no live activation is implied.
:::

## A driver or vehicle is unavailable
Check the operating region, duty status, roster, vehicle availability, maintenance and archived state. Review capacity for passengers and luggage. Check overlapping bookings with the configured scheduling buffer.

## A booking cannot be dispatched
Review the reservation status and quotation. Pending quotations block confirmation and dispatch; terminal bookings cannot be reopened through normal editing. Verify the account has dispatch permissions.

## The booking is missing from the calendar
Check date, hub, day/week/month view and status filter. Inspect the reservation directly before assuming it was removed. Do not create a duplicate.

## When to escalate
If resources are genuinely unavailable, reorganize assignments through authorized workflows. An override is not a substitute for safe capacity or operational readiness.
