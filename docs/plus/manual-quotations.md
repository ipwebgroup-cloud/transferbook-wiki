# Manual quotations

::: info Reference release
TransferBook+ v0.7.9 source baseline. This is a local review draft, not confirmation of production availability. Use only an authorized environment and verify the installed version and permissions.
:::

## When a manual quotation is required
An unconfigured route may create a new reservation with **Quote Pending**. Confirmation and chauffeur dispatch remain blocked until a valid EUR tariff is approved.

## Procedure (company administrator)
1. Open the pending reservation in Reservations and locate **Manual Quotation Required**.
2. Verify the requested service and determine the authorized commercial tariff according to your business policy.
3. Enter **Approved Total Tariff (EUR)** as a valid positive amount. Add **Quotation Note (Optional)** when useful.
4. Submit the approval through the form and verify the recorded tariff. The approval itself keeps the status **New**.
5. Follow the normal confirmation workflow when all conditions are met.

## Troubleshooting
If the approval form is unavailable, verify that the account has the company administrator role and that the reservation is still quote-pending. Do not use a fictitious tariff or mark the journey as free.
