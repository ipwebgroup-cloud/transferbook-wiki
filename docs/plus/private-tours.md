# Private tours catalogue

::: info Reference release
TransferBook+ v0.7.9 source-reviewed draft. Availability depends on the installed release, permissions and configuration; no commercial activation is asserted.
:::

Prepare and manage private-tour catalogue entries and their commercial details.

## Before you begin

Use an approved workspace, confirm your company and role, and use synthetic records for training.

## Workflow

1. Open the private tours workspace and filter by publication status or category, or search tours and destinations.
2. With tour-management permission, add or edit a tour. Review title, hub, category, duration, vehicle recommendation, itinerary, inclusions and optional extras.
3. Choose Fixed Tariff (EUR) or Bespoke Custom Quote as appropriate. Save using the intended publication status and review the resulting catalogue entry before using it in a booking.

## Verification and troubleshooting

Duplicating a tour or setting a publication status is not proof that a public booking channel is live. Check pricing and availability; do not accept a tour with an unresolved quotation.
