## Before you begin

Use an approved workspace, confirm your company and role, and use synthetic records for training.

## Workflow

1. Search partner records by hotel, concierge, contact person or hub. Filter by partner category and open the appropriate commercial profile.
2. With management permission, add or edit the partner name, category, region, contact details, commission and settlement method. Check whether the agreement is active or pending review.
3. Review contracted rates and commission entries against the agreed contract. Record settlement only when supported by actual evidence and authorized procedures.

## Verification and troubleshooting

A profile or configured commission does not prove a signed agreement or a paid invoice. Do not enter invented commercial terms or settle a disputed entry.
