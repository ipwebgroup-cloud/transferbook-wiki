## Before you begin

Use an approved workspace, confirm your company and role, and use synthetic records for training.

## Workflow

1. In Fleet & Luxury Vehicles Management, search by registration plate, make/model, chauffeur or hub. Filter by Available, On Trip, Maintenance or Offline.
2. With management permission, use Add Vehicle or Edit Vehicle. Select the operating region and vehicle class, enter the actual registration, make/model, year and passenger/luggage capacities.
3. Review vehicle class definitions before saving a record. Verify the resulting vehicle details and operational status. Use the reservation assignment workflow for a specific trip.

## Verification and troubleshooting

A vehicle class is not a vehicle. Do not inflate capacity or treat a linked chauffeur as proof of assignment to a reservation. If an operation is denied, check permissions or archived status.
