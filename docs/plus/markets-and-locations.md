# Markets, channels and locations

::: info Reference release
TransferBook+ v0.7.9 source-reviewed draft. Availability depends on the installed release, permissions and configuration; no commercial activation is asserted.
:::

Maintain the operating markets and location catalogue used by reservations and channel configuration.

## Before you begin

Use an approved workspace, confirm your company and role, and use synthetic records for training.

## Workflow

1. In System & Executive Settings, inspect the operating market before editing its catalogue. Confirm the active company and authorized hub.
2. Use the relevant market, channel or location editor. Review names, associations and active state; do not reuse a location from the wrong market.
3. Save only authorized changes and review the market/channel/location state. Verify that an intended location is selectable in the appropriate booking context using an approved test environment.

## Verification and troubleshooting

An enabled market or channel record is not sufficient to activate public intake. Archiving a market can affect downstream selection; review dependencies before changing state.
