## Purpose and access
The dispatch calendar provides day, week and month views for an authorized operator to review workload and scheduling.

## Procedure
1. Open **Dispatch Timeline & Daily Schedule** or the calendar view.
2. Choose day, week or month. Select the relevant date/period and operating hub.
3. Filter by **All statuses**, **Quote required**, **Tour acceptance pending**, **Dispatch review**, **Assigned / In transit**, or **Completed / Cancelled**.
4. Review scheduled pickups in chronological context. Check the booking detail before taking an assignment action.
5. Verify the driver, vehicle, capacity, region and overlapping work before dispatching through the authorized controls.

## Expected result
The selected period and filters display matching bookings. Review and quote-pending items are not equivalent to dispatch-ready bookings.

## Troubleshooting
If a trip is missing, check hub, period and status filter. If assignment is refused, resolve scheduling or capacity conflicts. See [Driver and vehicle assignment](/plus/assignments).
