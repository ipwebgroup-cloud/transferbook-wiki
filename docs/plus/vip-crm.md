## Before you begin

Use an approved workspace, confirm your company and role, and use synthetic records for training.

## Workflow

1. In VIP Clients & CRM Profiles, search by name, email or phone. Review duplicate indicators manually; matching email does not automatically merge profiles.
2. Open or edit the appropriate profile with permission. Record genuine contact details, languages and relevant service preferences; passenger email remains optional.
3. Review explicitly linked reservations. If needed, select an unlinked company reservation and link it after confirming identity. Review saved locations and consent before using customer data.

## Verification and troubleshooting

Never invent contact details or link a booking based only on a similar name. Privacy export or anonymization controls require authorized handling and policy review; do not test them on real customers.
