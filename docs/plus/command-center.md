## Before you begin

Use an approved workspace, confirm your company and role, and use synthetic records for training.

## Workflow

1. Review Operational attention queues before opening individual reservations. Prioritize exceptions and pending operational work.
2. Review Operational key metrics. Treat booked tariff value and collected revenue as different measures; do not assume a confirmed booking is paid.
3. Inspect Upcoming Airport & Port Arrivals, Chauffeurs & Fleet Standby and Latest Reservation Audit & Activity. Open the relevant reservation in the operator workspace for action.

## Verification and troubleshooting

If a queue or metric looks empty, check your company, hub, permissions and data mode before concluding there is no work. Dashboard figures are not proof of live GPS or a completed payment.
