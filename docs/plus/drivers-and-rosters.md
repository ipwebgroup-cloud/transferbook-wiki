## Before you begin

Use an approved workspace, confirm your company and role, and use synthetic records for training.

## Workflow

1. Open Chauffeurs & Crew Management. Search by name, licence, language or hub; filter by On Duty, Busy / On Transfer or Off Duty.
2. Use Add Chauffeur or Edit Chauffeur if authorized. Review the region, contact details, licence category and expiry, permit, languages and availability. Enter only verified information.
3. In the schedule area select a chauffeur, schedule type (Active Shift, Airport Standby, Day Off or Absence / Leave), dates and times. Add an optional roster note and review the saved block.

## Verification and troubleshooting

A roster entry is not a trip assignment. Check schedule conflicts separately; a profile is not proof of licence validity or account access. Do not fabricate contact or licence information.
