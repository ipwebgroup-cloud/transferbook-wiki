## Before you begin

Use an approved workspace, confirm your company and role, and use synthetic records for training.

## Workflow

1. In System & Executive Settings, verify Company Trading Name, Legal Corporate Entity, VAT / Tax Identification and dispatch contact details.
2. Review the configured night hours, surcharge, round-trip return discount, seasonal markup and Default Buffer (Minutes). Confirm commercial changes with the responsible administrator.
3. Review enabled languages and the default language. Use translation export/import and override controls only with permission. Check the resulting text in the intended locale; retain a known-good export before changes.

## Verification and troubleshooting

Do not change pricing defaults as a translation exercise. Translation changes do not create new functionality. The Wiki languages are English and Greek; application locale availability is separate.
