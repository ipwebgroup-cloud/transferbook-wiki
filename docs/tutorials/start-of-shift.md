# Start-of-shift dispatch checklist

::: info
Source-reviewed training draft · TransferBook+ v0.7.9. Verify installed release, permissions and configuration. Use authorized synthetic training data; no live activation is implied.
:::

## Training scenario
A dispatcher prepares for the day's arrivals and departures. Use the authorized company context and approved training data.

## Steps
1. Open Command Center and inspect Operational attention queues. Review upcoming airport and port arrivals.
2. Open the dispatch calendar and select Day, the correct date and operating hub. Review all statuses first, then filter for dispatch review or quote required.
3. Identify bookings missing assignments and those needing quotation or tour acceptance. Resolve commercial gates before dispatch.
4. Review driver duty status, roster, vehicle status and maintenance windows. Match passenger and luggage capacity and check overlap including the configured buffer.
5. Verify each saved assignment and revisit the attention queues. Share any unresolved exceptions with the responsible operator through approved channels.

## Expected result
A reviewed schedule with clear ownership of exceptions. The dashboard is not proof of live GPS or collected payments.
