## Scope

Persistent regulatory records and attempt history are implemented for sandbox preparation. Live regulatory submission is explicitly blocked in the inspected source. Neither a sandbox record nor a generated reference is an official submission, statutory document or compliance certification.

## Authorized sandbox workflow

1. Confirm the company, invoice or reservation, user permission and synthetic test environment.
2. For AADE/myDATA, prepare a sandbox record from an eligible invoice with the required invoice-write permission. Review the stored document and attempt history with the responsible accounting administrator.
3. For EDXEIX, use the authorized dispatch workflow to issue a sandbox record for the correct reservation and leg, and review supported extension/cancellation operations. Chauffeur access is restricted; do not use another chauffeur's assignment or company.
4. Verify the stored record and audit status. Do not represent a sandbox result as an official reference, permission to operate or tax submission.
5. If a live attempt is blocked, retain the recorded result and follow the approved manual statutory process. Do not bypass the block.

Before future live activation, confirm current official specifications, credentials, account ownership, required legal/accounting review, permissions, actual acceptance and operating procedure. No live credential recipe is approved for this release.
